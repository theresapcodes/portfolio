About

I’m a staff product designer who has led design teams while staying hands-on, from strategy through launch. I help startups and growing companies turn complex products into clear, cohesive customer experiences. Working closely with customers and internal teams, I uncover gaps, clarify product direction, and build design systems that support growth. My experience spans product strategy, UX, product design, design systems, brand, and marketing across B2B and B2B2C SaaS. I enjoy working through ambiguity and turning insights into practical solutions.

Lately, I’ve been exploring what’s next and how AI can strengthen my practice. I’ve been experimenting with connected workflows across Figma, MCP, Claude Code, and Cursor, while making space for creative side quests. If your team needs someone who can connect product strategy, UX, systems, and brand, let’s connect.

Let's build something great.
